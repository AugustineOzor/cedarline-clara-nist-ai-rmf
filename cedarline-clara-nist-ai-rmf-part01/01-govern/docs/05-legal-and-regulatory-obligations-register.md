## 1. Purpose

This register lists the laws, regulations, supervisory guidance and contract terms that apply to CLARA, with a named owner and the evidence that shows each obligation is met. It turns "we must comply" into specific, trackable actions.

## 5. How the register is maintained

- **Regulatory horizon scanning:** Compliance reviews new laws, guidance and enforcement every month and adds relevant items within 10 working days.
- **Evidence:** each obligation links to the evidence that proves it is met. An item is marked "Met" only when the evidence has been reviewed by its owner and by Compliance.
- **Legal review:** the wording of each obligation is a summary. Legal Counsel confirms interpretations against the official texts.
