## 1. Purpose

AI controls only work if the people around the system understand them. This plan sets who must be trained on AI risk, on what, how often, and how completion is recorded.

The EU AI Act's AI literacy provision (Article 4), as amended by the Digital Omnibus on AI, places less of a direct duty on organisations than the original text. Cedarline keeps a role-based AI literacy programme anyway, because effective human oversight and risk management depend on it.

## 2. Training by audience

| Audience | Approx. headcount | Module | Format | Frequency | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **All staff** | 3,400 | AI essentials: what AI is, approved tools, acceptable use, how to report concerns | 30-minute e-learning | On joining, then every year | 95% within 60 days |
| **Board and Executive Committee** | 20 | AI risk oversight: risk tolerance, regulatory exposure, questions to ask | 90-minute briefing | Every year | 100% |
| **AI Governance Committee** | 9 | AI governance in depth: gate decisions, impact assessment, risk acceptance | Half-day workshop | Every year | 100% |
| **Credit underwriters and credit officers** | 140 | Working with CLARA: reading reason codes, when and how to override, avoiding automation bias, escalation | 2-hour workshop with case studies | Before using CLARA, then every year | 100% before access is granted |
| **Model developers and data scientists** | 35 | Responsible model development: data governance, bias testing, explainability, documentation | 1-day course | On joining, then every two years | 100% |
| **Model Risk Management** | 12 | Validating machine learning models: fairness metrics, robustness, explainability tests | 1-day course | Every year | 100% |
| **Compliance, Legal and DPO teams** | 25 | AI regulation: EU AI Act, GDPR automated decisions, Consumer Credit Directive | Half-day workshop | Every year and on major change | 100% |
| **Customer complaints handlers** | 60 | Explaining AI-supported credit decisions and handling requests for human review | 1-hour module | Before handling CLARA cases, then every year | 100% |
| **Procurement and vendor managers** | 15 | Buying AI: due diligence questions and contract terms | 1-hour module | Every year | 100% |

## 3. Access depends on training

Underwriters and credit officers will not be given access to CLARA's review screens until they complete the "Working with CLARA" workshop and pass its assessment (pass mark 80%). Access is suspended automatically if annual refresher training is more than 30 days overdue.

## 4. Records

For every person, the learning system records the module, version, date completed, assessment score and next due date. Records are kept for at least seven years and are available to Internal Audit and regulators.

## 5. Measures

| Measure | Target | Reported to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Completion rate by audience | As in section 2 | AI Governance Committee, monthly |
| Underwriter assessment pass rate | At least 90% at first attempt | Head of Retail Credit Operations |
| Override rate trend after training | Within the 3% to 15% green band | AI Governance Committee |
| Staff who know how to report an AI concern (annual survey) | At least 85% | AI Governance Committee |

## 6. Review

Training content is reviewed every year, and after any serious AI incident, major regulatory change or significant change to CLARA.
